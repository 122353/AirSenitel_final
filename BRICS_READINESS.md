# BRICS Track 2 alignment

AirSentinel aligns with the Clean Air & Climate Resilience challenge by demonstrating a working India-first version of the required evidence-to-action loop:

- combines monitored pollutants, weather, satellite context and citizen evidence;
- moves from national/city overview to configured locality pilots;
- forecasts one, three and four hours ahead where the quality gate passes;
- identifies hidden coverage gaps and unexpected forecast residuals;
- preserves provenance, uncertainty, privacy and human authority review;
- uses interoperable canonical records so a future governed multi-country exchange is possible.

It does not claim a live BRICS network, cross-border data-sharing agreement or government partnership. The submission focuses on the safer working local-to-national platform and Google service integrations. Deep-learning and federated-learning models are not promoted.
